Tab Palette

Your tabs, one shortcut away.

Suggestions

Off by default. When enabled, the text you type is sent to Google’s autocomplete service. Your tab list, history, and bookmarks stay on this Mac. Submitting a search always uses your Chrome default search engine.

Keyboard

⌘T opens the native palette when the companion has Accessibility permission. Without it, New Tab briefly relays to a launcher window. ⌘⇧K and the toolbar button also open it.

Use ↑ ↓ to select, Return to open, and Esc to dismiss. Number and letter shortcuts jump to tabs; ⌘T toggles the palette closed.
